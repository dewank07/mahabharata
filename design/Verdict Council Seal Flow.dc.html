—Verdict · the Council Seal system, applied to the whole game

Every screen below is the 5a Council Seal language: flat blackened surfaces with paper grain, 1px rules at three weights, aged brass for rank and state, parchment reserved for what you act on. No gradients, no glow, no shadows.

Two changes to the table. The centre of the ring is now the seal itself — your emblem struck into the board — and everything that was competing with it (quest numeral, "II of II named", the second inscription band) moved to where it already belonged: the quest plate on the left and a single line above the action. The ring holds seats, and nothing else.

Flow, from the engine: lobby → reveal (night) → propose → vote → quest → (Lady of the Lake / Excalibur / plot windows) → assassin → end. Team sizes, the 5-rejection limit, the two-fail fourth quest at 7+, and the night script are taken from convex/logic.ts.

01Lobby — 15 in the roomphase: lobby · edge case

The engine seats at most ten (MAX_PLAYERS = 10), so fifteen arriving is a real state the UI has to answer rather than refuse. The seal fills with ten seats; everyone after that becomes a watcher in an ordered queue, promoted automatically when a seat empties, and the host can seat a watcher directly. The setup validator's message is shown verbatim where it blocks the start.
